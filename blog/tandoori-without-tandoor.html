Tandoori Flavour Without a Tandoor: A Simple Guide

By Flavoura1 October 20262 min read

You can get that smoky, bold tandoori taste on an ordinary pan or oven. The marinade does most of the work.

Steps

  1. Mix the tandoori premix with a little thick curd.
  2. Coat your paneer or vegetables and rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Cook on a hot pan or grill, turning until charred at the edges.
Tip: use a hot pan and don't overcrowd it, so the edges char instead of steaming.
