Ditch Takeout Apps: How Cooking Premixes Save Time and Money

By Flavoura5 October 20263 min read

It is 8:30 PM on a Tuesday. You are exhausted, staring at a food delivery app, and watching a single handi of Butter Chicken skyrocket from a ₹350 menu price to ₹490 after platform fees, packaging charges, GST, and delivery surcharges.

An hour later, your food arrives lukewarm, swimming in restaurant oil, and barely feeding two people. But what if you could cook a richer, restaurant-grade meal at home in just 15 minutes for half the price?

The Real Math: Delivery vs. Cooking Fresh

When you order takeout, you are paying for logistics, packaging, and app commissions—not just food quality. Here is how ordering out compares directly to cooking at home using a Flavoura Gourmet Premix pack:

  • Food Delivery App (~₹480 Total): ₹350 dish base + ₹130 in taxes, packaging, and platform fees. Yields 1–2 modest servings after a 45-to-60-minute wait.
  • Flavoura Home Meal (~₹290 – ₹340 Total): ₹120 Flavoura Premix + ₹150–₹200 for 500g fresh chicken or paneer + ₹20 for curd/puree. Yields 3–4 generous servings in just 15 minutes.

More Food, Better Ingredients, Zero Chopping

The biggest reason people default to takeout apps isn't the cooking itself—it is the prep work. Slicing onions, mincing garlic, and waiting for gravies to simmer takes time you don't have after a long workday.

Flavoura premixes eliminate 100% of the prep work. Premium spices, cashew bases, and aromatics are already slow-crafted inside the pack. All you do is add your protein (paneer, chicken, or veggies), a splash of milk, curd, or tomato puree, and pan-cook for 10 to 15 minutes.

Better Quality Control for Your Family

When you cook at home using instant premixes, you stay in total control of what goes into your body:

  • Fresh Protein: You select high-quality paneer or fresh chicken from your trusted local vendor.
  • Zero Preservatives: No artificial food colors, hidden MSG, or re-used commercial frying oils.
  • Hot & Fresh: Your meal is served straight from the pan to your plate—not steamed inside plastic containers in a delivery bag.
