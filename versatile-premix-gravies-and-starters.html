1 Pack, 2 Ways: How to Cook Rich Gravies OR Dry Seekh Kababs & Tikkas with Flavoura Premixes

By Flavoura5 October 20263 min read

Most people think of instant masala premixes as a one-trick pony—strictly meant for making main-course curries and gravies. But limiting a artisan blend to just gravy cooking misses half its potential.

Because every Flavoura Gourmet Premix is micro-milled with slow-roasted spices, aromatics, and finely ground nuts, it doubles as a dry marinade and seasoning base. Whether you want a velvet-smooth dinner gravy or a charred, dry seekh kabab starter, one packet handles both effortlessly.

Mode 1: The Classic Velvet Gravy (Main Course)

When you want a comforting, restaurant-style curry to pair with naan, roomali roti, or rice, liquid is your best friend:

  • The Method: Whisk 1 packet of Flavoura premix with 200g curd or tomato puree and a splash of water directly in your pan.
  • The Texture: As heat is applied, the ground cashews, melon seeds, and whole-roasted spices release natural oils, emulsifying into a rich, silky gravy.
  • Best For: Weeknight family dinners, Paneer Butter Masala, Kadhai Veg, or chicken gravies.

Mode 2: The Sizzling Dry Starter (Seekh Kababs, Tikkas & Air-Fryer Bites)

Craving a dry, spiced starter for game night, evening snacks, or a high-protein meal? Skip adding water or heavy liquids entirely!

Step 1 (The Dry Marinade): Mix 2–3 tablespoons of Flavoura premix directly with 2 spoons of thick hung curd (or 1 tbsp mustard oil/lemon juice) to form a thick, concentrated paste.

Step 2 (Coat Your Protein): Coat paneer cubes, soya chunks, mushroom caps, or minced chicken/mutton for seekh kababs thoroughly in the mix.

Step 3 (Pan-Fry, Grill, or Air-Fry): Sear in a hot skillet with a dab of butter or air-fry at 180°C for 8–10 minutes until you get a crisp, smoky outer crust.

Why Flavoura Premixes Work So Well for Dry Starters

Standard store-bought spice powders often burn or turn bitter when exposed to direct skillet searing or air-frying without liquid. Flavoura premixes solve this through precise milling and roasting:

  • Zero Raw Taste: The whole spices and aromatics are slow-roasted before grinding, meaning they don't need prolonged liquid simmering to release their peak flavor.
  • Natural Crust Coating: The finely milled cashew and seed base forms a golden, caramelized crust around paneer cubes or kebabs, locking in moisture while delivering a smoky crunch.
  • Perfect Salt & Heat Balance: No need to guess how much salt or chilli powder to add to your tikka marinade—everything is pre-proportioned for foolproof seasoning.

Unlocking Double Duty in Your Kitchen

Keeping a few packs of Flavoura premixes in your pantry gives you complete menu flexibility. Cook a comforting gravy dish on Monday, and turn the same pack into sizzling paneer tikkas or seekh kababs on Friday night.
